This Privacy Policy explains how Find My Local ("we", "us", the "App"), operated by FINDMYLOCAL PRIVATE LIMITED (CIN: U82990TS2026PTC217408), Flat 204, Sri Srinivasa Nilayam Apartments, Road No. 18, Vishnupuri Colony, Peerzadiguda, Hyderabad, Telangana - 500098, India, collects, uses, shares, and protects your information when you use our mobile application connecting Users with local Helpers across India. Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act") we are the Data Fiduciary and you are the Data Principal.

Lawful basis. We process personal data only for a lawful purpose. Depending on the purpose, processing may rest on your consent, a specified legitimate use under the DPDP Act, compliance with a legal obligation, or another basis permitted by law. Consent is not bundled with unrelated purposes, and withdrawal of consent is as easy as giving it. The App is for adults aged 18 and above.

1. Information We Collect

a. Account & profile information

  • All accounts: mobile phone number, name, date of birth (to confirm you meet the 18+ age requirement), gender, profile photo, and location details (village, district, state, PIN code, languages).
  • Helper accounts additionally: skills/services offered, bio, pricing, service locations, and — for verification — a live selfie, government ID proof (e.g. Aadhaar, Voter ID, Driving Licence), and address proof. If you choose Aadhaar, upload a Masked Aadhaar (the app asks for this): we do not need or store your full Aadhaar number, and we do not perform UIDAI e-KYC or Aadhaar authentication — verification is a visual document review only. We do not create biometric templates or run facial recognition on the selfie. Helpers may also add payment details (UPI ID, UPI QR image, bank account number, IFSC, bank name, account-holder name) and gallery photos. Payment details are stored in a private area only you can access — our staff cannot view them through any administrative tool, and they reach a User only when you yourself send them in a chat.
  • Verification-document lifecycle: once verification is APPROVED, the uploaded document images are automatically deleted within 24 hours of the decision; if REJECTED, they are kept up to 90 days for appeal/resubmission, then deleted; undecided submissions are deleted after 180 days. We retain only the decision, its date, the reviewer, and the document type — never the document image or its number — unless an active investigation or legal preservation duty requires longer retention.

b. Location information

  • Precise location to find nearby Helpers, set your service area, and share your location in chat. Others never see your precise location — search results show only distance bands.
  • Background location — only when you start a "Live Location" share in a chat, and only with the single chat counterparty you choose, for the duration you pick (up to 24 hours). The sharing continues in the background so that one person can follow your journey in real time; you can stop it at any time, and it ends automatically when the time expires.

c. Camera, photos & contacts

  • Camera & photo library: to capture your verification selfie/documents, set a profile picture, add gallery photos, and share images in chat.
  • Contacts: accessed only at the moment you choose to share a contact in chat; we do not upload or store your full address book.

d. Content you create

  • Chat messages, shared images and locations; service commitment (agreement) details; reviews and ratings; Work Posts; and emergency/missing-person/vehicle alerts you create.

e. Device, security & usage information

  • Device model/OS, app version, a push-notification token, diagnostic/crash data, and product-usage analytics (which screens and features are used) to improve reliability and the experience.
  • Device-attestation signal (Android): we use the Google Play Integrity API and Firebase App Check to confirm that sign-in and OTP requests come from a genuine, unmodified build of our App on a genuine device, which prevents SMS-pumping and bot abuse. We receive only a pass/fail verdict; the raw device data is processed by Google and is not stored by us.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage your account and verify Helper identities for community trust and safety.
  • Show nearby Helpers, enable chat, service agreements, reviews, Work Posts, and (Helper-initiated) sharing of payment details between you and the other party.
  • Send notifications about chats, requests, agreements, alerts, and account/verification status.
  • Broadcast regional emergency/missing-person/vehicle alerts to relevant users.
  • Maintain security, prevent fraud and abuse, moderate content (including optional AI-assisted screening with human review), and comply with law.
  • Diagnose crashes and analyse usage to improve the App.

AI-assisted moderation. Reported or automatically flagged content may be processed by an AI moderation provider (currently Google Gemini) to generate a policy-risk signal. The AI receives only the flagged content, never your name, phone number, or account identity. AI output is never the sole basis for a suspension, ban, or trust-score change — a trained human reviewer assesses the facts, and you can appeal in-app.

4. Data Retention

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account, your profile and personal records are removed (see how to delete your account). Content shared with another party (e.g. messages already delivered, reviews on other profiles) may persist in that party's records, and limited data may be retained where required for legal, security (including a minimum of 180 days of authentication logs under the CERT-In Directions, 2022), fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution purposes. Accounts inactive for 24 consecutive months may be deleted after at least 30 days' prior notice.

5. Your Rights & Choices (DPDP Act)

  • Access: obtain a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we process it.
  • Correction & updating: view and edit your profile in the App at any time, or request a correction.
  • Erasure: delete your account and personal data in-app via Profile → Delete Account, or use our account-deletion page. Deleting your account exercises this right in full, except for data we must retain by law.
  • Nomination: nominate a person to exercise your rights on your behalf in the event of death or incapacity.
  • Withdrawal of consent: withdraw consent at any time (by disabling device permissions, contacting us, or deleting your account); this does not affect processing already lawfully carried out.
  • Grievance redressal: contact our Grievance Officer below; if unsatisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

6. Security

We use industry-standard measures (encrypted transport, access-controlled cloud storage, per-role access rules, single-active-session enforcement, and audit logging) to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work continuously to safeguard your information, and we will notify you and the relevant authorities of a reportable breach as required by law.

7. Age Requirement & Children

The App is for adults only — both Helpers and Users must be at least 18 years old. Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, any individual under 18 is a "child"; because the App is restricted to adults, we do not knowingly create accounts for, or process the personal data of, any child, and we carry out no tracking, behavioural monitoring, or targeted advertising directed at children. If we learn we hold the data of a person under 18, we suspend the account and delete the data (subject to any legal or safety hold), and a parent or guardian may contact our Grievance Officer to have it reviewed and deleted.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be notified in-app or by updating the version and "Effective" date above. Where a change requires fresh consent under law, we will ask for it.
