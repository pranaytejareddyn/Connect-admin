1. Information We Collect

a. Account & profile information

  • All accounts: mobile phone number, name, age/date of birth, gender, profile photo, and location details (village, district, state, PIN code, languages).
  • Helper accounts additionally: skills/services offered, bio, pricing, service locations, and — for verification — a live selfie, government ID proof (e.g. Aadhaar, Voter ID, Driving Licence), and address proof. Helpers may also add payment details (UPI ID, UPI QR image, bank account number, IFSC, bank name, account-holder name) and gallery photos.

b. Location information

  • Precise location to find nearby Helpers, set your service area, and share your location in chat.
  • Background location — only when you start a "Live Location" share in a chat, and only for the duration you choose — so the person you're chatting with can see your live location. You can stop it at any time.

c. Camera, photos & contacts

  • Camera & photo library: to capture your verification selfie/documents, set a profile picture, add gallery photos, and share images in chat.
  • Contacts: accessed only at the moment you choose to share a contact in chat; we do not upload or store your full address book.

d. Content you create

  • Chat messages, shared images, locations, and payment cards; service commitment (agreement) details; reviews and ratings; and emergency/missing-person/vehicle alerts you create.

e. Device & usage information

  • Device model/OS, app version, a push-notification token, diagnostic/crash data, and product-usage analytics (which screens and features are used) to improve reliability and the experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage your account and verify Helper identities for community trust and safety.
  • Show nearby Helpers, enable chat, service agreements, reviews, and (Helper-initiated) sharing of payment details between you and the other party.
  • Send notifications about chats, requests, agreements, alerts, and account/verification status.
  • Broadcast regional emergency/missing-person/vehicle alerts to relevant users.
  • Maintain security, prevent abuse, moderate content, and comply with law.
  • Diagnose crashes and analyse usage to improve the App.

4. Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account (see below), your profile and personal records are removed. Content shared with another party (e.g. chat history, agreements) may persist in that party's records, and limited data may be retained where required for legal, security, or dispute-resolution purposes.

5. Your Rights & Choices

  • Access & correction: view and edit your profile in the App at any time.
  • Deletion: delete your account and personal data in-app via Profile → Delete Account. You may also email us to request deletion.
  • Permissions: control location, camera, photos, contacts, and notifications from your device Settings.
  • Grievance/Data-protection contact: reach us at the email below; we aim to respond within the timelines required by the India DPDP Act, 2023.

6. Security

We use industry-standard measures (encrypted transport, access-controlled cloud storage) to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work continuously to safeguard your information.

7. Children

Helpers must be aged 18 or above. Users must be at least 13; we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, any individual under 18 is a "child". For Users aged 13 to 17, we process personal data only with the verifiable consent of a parent or legal guardian, and we do not carry out tracking, behavioural monitoring, or targeted advertising directed at children. A parent or legal guardian may contact our Grievance Officer to review, correct, or delete their child's data. If we learn that we have collected a child's data without the required consent, we will delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be notified in-app or by updating the "Last updated" date above.
