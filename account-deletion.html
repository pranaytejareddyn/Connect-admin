Delete Your Account & Data

Find My Local (com.connectapp.india) · FINDMYLOCAL PRIVATE LIMITED · Last updated 7 November 2026

This page explains how to delete your Find My Local account and the personal data associated with it. Find My Local is operated by FINDMYLOCAL PRIVATE LIMITED (CIN: U82990TS2026PTC217408), Hyderabad, Telangana, India.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open the Find My Local app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Open My Profile (or Settings).
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm. Your account and associated personal data are deleted.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you can no longer sign in, email us from the phone number/email on your account and we will verify your identity and delete your account on your behalf:

Email: support@findmylocal.co or grievance@findmylocal.co
Subject: Account Deletion Request
Please include the mobile number registered on your account so we can verify and locate it.

We acknowledge requests within 24 hours and complete verified deletions within the timelines required by the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What gets deleted

When your account is deleted, we delete or anonymise the personal data associated with it, including:

  • Your user and/or helper profile (name, photo, location, bio, skills, pricing);
  • Your phone-number sign-in record;
  • Private payment details you saved (UPI ID, QR image, bank account, IFSC);
  • Identity and address verification documents (if not already purged by our verification-retention schedule);
  • Gallery and profile photos (permanently removed from storage);
  • Notification history, saved lists, blocks, and your push-notification token;
  • Your device's local app cache.

Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone. You will lose access to your profile, verification status, chat history, and any saved data that is not required to be retained by law.
